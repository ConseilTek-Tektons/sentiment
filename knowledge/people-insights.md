# Sentiment people-insights guide

## Anonymity

Release a cohort score only when the respondent count meets the configured threshold after every active filter. The product minimum is five. A masked result should not include the hidden average or raw response detail.

## Interpreting engagement

Treat a change as a signal for investigation, not proof of causation. Compare question wording, participation, cohort composition, seasonality, and confidence before recommending action. Avoid ranking individual managers from small or unstable cohorts.

## Action planning

Good action plans name an owner, a small observable change, a due date, and a follow-up measure. Prefer one or two credible commitments over a long list with no accountability.

## Free-text responses

Remove unnecessary identifiers before analysis. Do not quote text that could identify a respondent. Human reviewers remain responsible for sensitive topics, employment decisions, and escalation paths.
